Privacy Policy

DrawPulse, by Joseph Huntley / CapableProcess.ai. Effective November 7, 2026.

The short version

DrawPulse does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings and the lottery results the app downloads are saved on your device only. Numbers you check are not sent anywhere.

Lottery results

To get new results, the app connects to the New York State open data website, data.ny.gov. As with any website, that service can see your device's internet address when it answers. We do not receive that information.

Children

DrawPulse is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@capableprocess.ai.

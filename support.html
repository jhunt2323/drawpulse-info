Why don't I see the latest draw?

New York usually posts results overnight, so recent draws may not appear yet. The bottom of each game shows how current its results are. Pull down to check for new draws.

What do hot and cold mean?

They compare how often a number or combination came up with what chance alone would produce over the same draws. They describe the past only. Every draw is random, so DrawPulse can't improve your odds of winning.

Is DrawPulse connected to the New York Lottery?

No. DrawPulse is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the New York Lottery or the New York State Gaming Commission. It doesn't sell tickets or take bets. Always confirm results with the official New York Lottery.
